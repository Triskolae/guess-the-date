About the game

Guess the Date is a game where your goal is to discover when different objects were created.

How does it work?

Each round presents you with an object. Your goal is to estimate the year it was created and get as close as possible to the correct date.

Use your knowledge, observation and intuition to improve your score and become better at placing objects in their historical context.

Discover history

From everyday objects to remarkable inventions, Guess the Date lets you explore different periods of history through the objects that define them.

Every object is an opportunity to discover something new.

Made for curious minds

Guess the Date is designed for anyone who enjoys history, objects and discovering where they fit in time.

You don't need to be a history expert. Trust your intuition, make your guess and learn as you play.
