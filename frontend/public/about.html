About the game

Explore history, one object at a time.

Guess the Date is a general knowledge game where your goal is to identify when an object was created.

How does it work?

Each round presents you with an object. Enter the year you think it was created and use the feedback and clues to get closer to the correct date.

The goal is not only to find the right answer, but also to develop a better sense of chronology as you play.

Learn while playing

From ancient objects to modern inventions, Guess the Date invites you to explore different periods of history through the objects that shaped them.

Progress through historical eras, discover rare objects and unlock achievements along the way.

Made for curious minds

Guess the Date is designed to make culture and history approachable through observation, intuition and discovery.

No history degree required — just take a guess and see where it takes you.
